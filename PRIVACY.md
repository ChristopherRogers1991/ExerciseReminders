# Privacy Policy

## Data Retention

This app collects no data about the user.
Exercises/Tags created in the app are only stored locally on the device.
There are no ads, trackers, etc.

## Data Deletion

There is no process for requesting the deletion of your data, because your data never
leaves your device. You can use your device's controls, e.g. clearing the app cache or
data, or deleting the app, to manage your local data.
